# tp

WearTwin: A Smart Wearable Device for Soldier Health, Fatigue and Readiness Monitoring

The project focuses on continuously monitoring soldiers during physically demanding and high-stress situations. We will combine multiple physiological and environmental parameters to get a complete picture of the soldier’s condition.

We will develop a wearable device using ESP32 that collects data from ECG, PPG/SpO₂, respiration, skin temperature and an IMU for body movement, along with environmental temperature and humidity. The data will be transmitted wirelessly to a computing system, where signal processing and AI will combine these parameters to estimate conditions such as physical fatigue, thermal strain, cardiovascular/respiratory stress and overall operational readiness.

These continuously updated parameters will form a Soldier Digital Twin, which represents the soldier’s current physiological state and how it changes over time. A dashboard will allow a medic or commander to monitor individual soldiers and identify those who may require rest, rotation or medical attention.

Our primary use case will be the early detection of physiological and thermal strain during strenuous activities, with further applications in fatigue monitoring, squad-level readiness assessment, casualty prioritization
